The Candela Group unites people affected by mental illness, and the families who love them, with the local care and resources that make lasting recovery possible.

Our purpose

Uniting people with the care they deserve

We bring stability and an improved quality of life to clients and their loved ones affected by mental illness by connecting them with public and private mental health services across Ventura County. We meet people where they are, and stay with them through the system's hardest turns.

How we help

Advocates who have been there

We're professional advocates with lived experience. We help clients and families navigate the mental health system with confidence, make informed choices, and move toward stability across ten essential areas of life.
