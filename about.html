About

About the Candela Group

Behavioral health advocates based in Ventura County, grounded in lived experience and committed to helping people access the care they deserve.

Our purpose

Uniting people with the care they deserve

The Candela Group's mission is to bring stability and an improved quality of life to the people we serve, and the families who love them, by uniting them with public and private mental health services across our community. Grounded in our own lived experience, we provide advocacy, education, guidance, and support for lasting recovery, and we treat everyone we work with dignity, respect, and confidentiality.

Our story

How we began

The Candela Group was formed as a California nonprofit public benefit corporation on October 25, 2021, and was approved as a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) organization by the IRS on July 21, 2022. We serve Ventura County and the surrounding region, guided by a simple belief: everyone deserves a clear path to quality care.

Our board

Board of Directors

The Candela Group is governed by a five-member volunteer Board of Directors.

Chris Tejeda

Chief Executive Officer, Founding Board Member

Chris began his advocacy as a consumer navigating the mental health system, and now leads the Candela Group as its Chief Executive Officer. He served on Ventura County's Behavioral Health Advisory Board from 2021 to 2025.
